# Billing & Payments

## Refund policy
Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds are returned to the original payment method and take 3-5 business days to appear after approval. Digital add-ons that were already used are refunded pro rata.

## Double charges
If a customer was charged twice for the same order, the duplicate charge is refunded automatically once our payment provider confirms it, usually within 3-5 business days. Orders with a duplicate charge show the status refund_pending. Customers do not need to contact their bank.

## Invoices
Invoices are available under Settings > Billing > Invoices and can be downloaded as PDF. Company details (VAT ID, address) can be edited before the invoice is generated; issued invoices cannot be changed, but a corrected invoice can be requested from support.

## Subscription changes
Plans can be upgraded at any time and the price difference is charged pro rata. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. Cancelling stops renewal; access continues until the period ends.
