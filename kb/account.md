# Account & Security

## Password reset
Use "Forgot password" on the login page. The reset link is valid for 1 hour. Support agents can never see or set passwords and will never ask for them.

## Two-factor authentication
2FA can be enabled under Settings > Security using an authenticator app. If a customer lost their 2FA device, they can sign in with one of their backup codes. Without backup codes, identity must be verified through the account recovery form, which takes up to 2 business days.

## Changing email address
The email can be changed under Settings > Profile. A confirmation link is sent to the new address and the change applies only after it is clicked.

## Account deletion
Accounts can be deleted under Settings > Privacy > Delete account. Deletion is permanent after a 14-day grace period, during which the customer can log in to cancel it.
