# General Questions

## Support hours
Human support is available Monday to Friday, 09:00-18:00 CET. Urgent issues outside these hours are handled by the on-call team.

## Product features
The product supports dark mode (Settings > Appearance), exports to CSV and PDF, and is available on web, iOS and Android. Feature requests can be submitted through the in-app feedback form and are reviewed monthly.

## Feedback
We read every piece of feedback. Customers are not individually notified when a request ships, but releases are announced in the changelog.
